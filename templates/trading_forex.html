Forex

EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY. Predicta shows how risky each pair is right now and how far to put a stop. Practice-account prices only. Nothing is bought for you.

Up or down? Predicta has no reliable call on that. We tested 15 years of prices, interest rates, fear, oil and the dollar, and nothing beat guessing after trading costs. So there is no "buy now" or "sell now" signal here, on purpose. You can still use the risk level and the stop to size a trade you choose yourself.
