UFC Analysis

ESPN fight history (real win/loss record + finish method, aggregated from event results) + FightMatrix ranking, blended with Glicko-2 (weight-class scoped, uncertainty-aware) — plus a separate method-of-victory model for KO/TKO, submission, and decision odds.

v1 model — league-average finish rates, blend weights, and the probability cap are provisional starting estimates, not yet backtested against real UFC results. Switched off ufcstats.com (JS anti-bot challenge) and Tapology (Cloudflare-blocked); ESPN's own fighter-bio endpoint is also confirmed dead for MMA, so reach/age aren't available from any live source — the model leans on ranking + Glicko-2 + real fight-history finish rates instead. Ring-rust (days since last fight) is not modeled yet. Treat outputs as directional until calibrated.
